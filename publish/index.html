Publish with us

You don’t have to wait until college to publish a book.

We only publish writers who haven’t started college. If you’re in middle school, in high school, or taking a gap year, you can send us your work. It costs you nothing.

Who can submit

  • Students in middle school or high school
  • Gap year students who haven’t enrolled in college yet
  • Writers under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign with them

How a book gets made

  1. 1

    Send your manuscript

    Send the full draft, or a sample and a short description of the book. Tell us your school year so we know you’re eligible.

  2. 2

    Editing

    An editor works through the manuscript with you, first the big questions of structure, then line by line.

  3. 3

    Design and production

    We lay out the interior, design the cover, and check the print proofs with you before anything goes out.

  4. 4

    Release

    Your book goes on sale on Amazon and in print, with your name on the cover.

What it costs, what you keep

Free

Authors never pay us. Editing, cover design, layout, and publishing cost you nothing.

You keep the copyright

Your book is copyrighted in your name, and your name is on its copyright page.

We publish it for three years

You give us the exclusive right to publish your book in print and as an ebook for three years from its release. After that it continues until either of us chooses to end it.

Other rights stay yours

Audiobook, translation, film, and TV rights belong to you. We don’t take a share of them.

You can take it back

If your book goes off sale, ask and we’ll return your publishing rights.

A parent signs too

Writers under 18 sign the publishing agreement together with a parent or guardian.
