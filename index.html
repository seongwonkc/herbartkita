Why we started

In high school, Jiwon Hur’s best writing kept getting dismissed as “by a kid.”

Herbart Kita is the publisher she wanted back then. We take manuscripts from writers who haven’t started college and give them the same editing, design, and care an adult author would get. Then we publish them.

The Argosy

Students from schools around the world run The Argosy. They pitch the stories, report them, and edit each other. We pay for it so they can write about what matters to them without chasing clicks. Its founding editor-in-chief, Minwoo Jung, has published three books with us.
