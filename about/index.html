About

A small publishing house for young writers.

What we are

Herbart Kita Publishing is an independent publisher in Gimpo, Korea. We publish only writers who haven’t started college yet, from middle school through the gap year. So far that means 9 books by 4 authors: an illustrated trilogy, a memoir, a civics guide, a book on Korean art and politics, political fables, and more.

Students do more than write here. They edit, proofread, design, and publish through our internships, and they report and edit The Argosy, the international student newspaper we fund.

Founders

Jiwon Hur 허지원

Co-founder · Representative
