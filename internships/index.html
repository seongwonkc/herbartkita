Student internships

Learn publishing by making books.

Our interns work on the books we publish. You pick a track, work with our editors, and leave with published work you helped make.

Pay
Unpaid
Where
Remote or hybrid
Time
10+ hours a week

Tracks

Editorial

Read submissions, write reader reports, and work with authors on revisions.

Copyediting & proofreading

Check manuscripts for grammar, consistency, and facts, then read the proofs before print.

Writing

Write jacket copy, author bios, and pieces that help each book find readers.

Book production

Help lay out interiors, work on covers, and prepare files for Amazon and the printer.

Publishing

Set up book listings, plan launches, and track how books reach readers.

The Argosy

Report and edit for The Argosy, the student newspaper we sponsor.

How to apply

Email seongwonkc@gmail.com with the track you want, your school year, how many hours a week you can give (10 at minimum), whether you want to work remotely or hybrid, and a short writing sample.
